## Per boss

### Chomp Frog (z1_t1) - Accept
Matches: squat, no neck, eyes on top with stepped brow, wide open mouth with pink tongue, cream belly, green block
fists, yellow wrist bands, toes. Reads at 4 studs.
- NOTE: the yellow band sits as a flat plate on the inner side of each fist; in the picture it wraps the wrist as a
  ring. Make it a full ring 2 cubes wide around the wrist.
- NOTE: fists sit out to the sides; the picture holds them forward at the chin. The guard pose can do this (animator),
  no model change needed.

### Snap Scorpion (z2_t2) - Accept with fixes
Matches: low orange body, tan legs with brown tips, banded tail curling up and over to a red hooked stinger, face on
the front of the body, yellow claw bands.
- SHOULD: the claws are round lumps with red bits. The picture's are curled open pincers (a C shape with a clear gap,
  red tips meeting). Build each claw as two jaws, upper bigger, with a 2-cube gap between them; red on the last 3
  cubes of each jaw.
- SHOULD: an orange carapace slab overhangs the face, so the brow and the top of the eyes are hidden; the picture's
  face has its own raised brow. Pull the carapace back 2 cubes and give the face a stepped brow above the eyes.
- NOTE: body orange is pale and peachy against the picture's saturated orange, with heavy cube-by-cube scatter. Sample
  the main orange again from the picture and keep the scatter subtle (TEAM CALL M2-24-1).
- NOTE: the tail is a little thin; the picture's segments are chunky (each about as thick as the claw band).

### Bit Shark (z3_t3) - Fix first
Matches: blue body, white belly running up into the jaw, eye with brow, big blue fists, standing on its legs and tail.
- MUST: no dorsal fin in the silhouette. The two dark-blue bumps on the head read as ears, so the build reads as a blue
  bear or cat. Remove both bumps; add one tall swept-back dorsal fin on the upper back behind the head (about 6 cubes
  tall, raked back, as in the picture), visible from the front and at the sheet angle.
- MUST: the mouth. The picture's mouth takes most of the head: a white jaw outline with a row of big square teeth on
  top and bottom and red inside. The build's lower jaw hangs as a white bib with a big pink tongue and few teeth.
  Rebuild: open mouth about half the head's height, 5 to 6 square white teeth top and bottom (2 cubes tall each),
  red-crimson inside, tongue small and low.
- SHOULD: the colour scatter is heavy (dark-blue patches over the fists and legs read as camouflage). Picture: one
  bright blue with a lighter shade. Keep two close blues (TEAM CALL M2-24-1).
- SHOULD: pectoral fins behind the fists and the tail fin behind the legs (both in the picture) are not visible.
  Add a small fin on the back of each forearm and a tail fin that shows beside a leg.

### Frost Byte (z4_t4) - Fix first
Matches: white-lavender fur with scattered shades, blue face plate, big blue fists, blue feet with toes, hulking build.
- MUST: eyes are black slots with no white. The creature rule and the picture: white eyes, at least 3 x 3 cubes, ink
  pupil. Replace with white eyes and a 2 x 2 ink pupil each, under the stepped blue brow.
- MUST: the mouth reads as a grille (a dark-red box with tiny white checks). The picture: a big open mouth with fangs
  on top, teeth below, pink tongue, dark inside. Rebuild: open mouth with 4 to 5 square top teeth (two longer fangs),
  3 to 4 bottom teeth, pink tongue, dark red behind.
- SHOULD: add the picture's blue muzzle and nose block between the eyes and the mouth (the picture names it; one
  block, 2 cubes wide).
- NOTE: fists hang at the hips; the picture holds them at the chest. Guard pose (animator), not the model.

### Ember Drake (z5_t5) - Fix first
Matches: bright red, cream-gold belly plates, yellow horns, red-bone wings with yellow membrane, big red fists, cream
claws on the feet, lava on chest, legs and fists, no flame anywhere (pack rule kept).
- MUST: the head. At the review angle the face reads flat like a cat or frog: eyes far apart on the sides, a wide
  mouth across the front. The picture's dragon has a long snout pointing forward and a bit to the side, the eye on top
  behind the brow, the jaw jutting with teeth along it. Make the snout about 1.5 times as long as it is wide and
  narrower than the skull, eyes set back on the top-sides behind a stepped brow, nostrils on the tip, teeth along the
  jaw line; turn the head 15 to 20 degrees toward the camera side as in the picture if the moves allow (check with the
  animator).
- MUST: the rock club reads as a loose cannonball on the ground: the tail behind is hidden, and the club is a round
  ball. The picture's tail is a big S sweeping out to one side; it should be seen. Lift the tail so it curves out
  from the rump and is visible beside the legs; make the club an angular rock (flat faces, 2 shades of basalt
  `#3E3842` with a lighter top), about the size of one fist, held off the ground at the tail's end.
- SHOULD: the lava on the fists and arms reads as yellow blobs and letter shapes. The picture's are thin jagged lines.
  Draw the cracks one cube wide, zig-zag, joined into lines that run across the fist and down the arm and leg. Check
  the glow share stays under 10%.
- NOTE: horn shape is right; the picture's horns curve back more.

### Star Golem (z6_t6) - Fix first
Matches: huge purple body, gold pauldrons, yoke, belt with octagon buckle, knee bands, gold boots, small head, tilted
gold halo, oversized purple fists with gold wrist bands.
- MUST: the face. The picture: glowing yellow slit eyes under a heavy angry purple brow, an open pink mouth. The build:
  a row of white teeth on dark that reads as a robot grin, and eyes too small to find. Make two yellow slit eyes 4 x 1
  to 5 x 2 cubes (Neon, the one boss allowed glowing eyes), a stepped brow 2 cubes thick over them, and an open mouth
  with pink inside and a few small teeth.
- MUST: the stars. The build has many large flat pale-yellow plus signs spread evenly over body, legs, pauldrons and
  fists; it reads as a polka pattern. The picture has a few medium stars and many tiny glowing specks on darker
  purple, and one big bright chest star. Keep about 8 medium stars (5-cube plus) and replace the rest with single-cube
  specks; colour them the saturated gold-yellow of the picture (Neon in Studio); make the chest star the largest and
  brightest. Stay inside the Mythic 15%.
- SHOULD: proportions are stockier than the picture: legs too short, the torso a box. Lengthen the legs about 15%
  and taper the torso from the shoulders to the belt (the picture's V).
- SHOULD: purple reads lighter and more scattered than the picture's deep violet. Darken the base purple toward the
  picture's sample.
- NOTE: the floating gold star cubes are the aura effect (section 5), not the model. Confirm with the manager at the
  review, as section 13 says.

## The six together
- **Cube size:** consistent; grows with tier as the table says. Pass.
- **Palette:** each boss uses its picture's colours. Pass, with the scatter below.
- **Colour scatter:** not one style yet. The frog and yeti use a light scatter; the shark, scorpion, drake and golem
  use a heavy one that reads as camouflage. Apply TEAM CALL M2-24-1 to all six.
- **Face language:** not one style yet. Frog, scorpion, shark and drake have white eyes with pupils and pink-inside
  mouths; the yeti has black slots and a grille, the golem a grille with no readable eyes. The fixes above bring all
  six to the picture's face rule.
- **Fists:** all six are rounded lumps of small cubes. In the picture each fist is a squarer block with a flat front
  and three or four clear finger rows. SHOULD, all six (frog last): give each fist a flat front face with 3 to 4
  finger rows split by a darker cube seam, and square the outline. The fists are the game's main read.

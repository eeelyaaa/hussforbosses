## One line
Steal-style lane runner and base-collector tycoon, up to 8 players a server, 20-minute sessions: time your "huss huss huss" punches on a bag to build Power, run down a six-zone lane to out-punch a voxel boss, carry it home past the zone hazards, and put it on a spot where it earns for you until somebody steals it.

Concept in one sentence, for every role: **punch the bag to get strong, beat a boss down the lane, and carry it home to earn for you.**

## Who it is for
- **Audience:** Roblox players of roughly 8 to 16 who already play "Steal a [Thing]" and simulator games. Bright, loud, readable.
- **Devices:** phone first (landscape, touch, weak devices). Tablet, PC and gamepad all work; nothing needs a keyboard.
- **Solo or friends:** playable alone from the first second. Other players are the danger and the fun: they steal from your base and you steal from theirs. Friends can join each other's server.
- **Sits next to:** base-stealing collector games (technique used: a base with a timed lock, carry-to-steal), punching and strength simulators (technique: train a stat, gates of bigger numbers), and rhythm-tap minigames (technique: a closing ring). Technique only. No game's names, layouts, creatures, assets or scripts are copied.
- **Fairness line (manager, 2026-11-25):** paid advantages are allowed in this game, including ones that work against other players, and attacking products are sold (a shield breaker among them). What is bought with Robux is kept through rebirth by its buyer. Bosses bought with Robux can be stolen like any other, as in the genre's leading games (manager, 2026-11-25), except where Roblox's paid-item trading flag forbids it (system 10). Nothing essential is behind a purchase: every zone, every boss and rebirth can be reached without paying.
- **Re-skin rule (manager, 2026-11-25: "we can replicate the game in different style"):** all rules in this brief refer to content by id (`zone 1..6`, `tier 1..6`, creature `z3_t3`, `hazard 1..6`). Names, models, colours, icons and sounds live in one skin table. A second skin swaps that table and the art; it changes no rule and no number. The second skin is not designed here.

## What the player does
| Timescale | What happens |
|---|---|
| Every few seconds (the action) | Taps the Huss button once as the timing ring closes, and the avatar fires off three quick punches, "huss huss huss", at the rhythm of the manager's recording; then the next ring. On the bag each landed punch adds Power. In a fight each landed punch takes health off the boss. While carrying, dodges the zone's moving hazard. At base, walks over cash pads. |
| Every few minutes (the loop) | One trip: punch the bag until a boss's number is within reach, run down the lane, win the fight, carry the boss home past the hazards, place it on a spot, collect cash, spend it on Punch Strength, Punch Speed or a boss upgrade, raise the lock shield before leaving again. |
| A session (20 minutes) | Minute 1: first boss placed and earning. Minute 5: first zone 2 trip, 4 or 5 bosses earning, first look into another player's base. Minute 10: a rare boss seen with a red number; spots filling; first steal attempt, given or taken. Minute 20: zone 3 or 4, all spots full, choosing which bosses to keep and timing the shield around longer trips. |
| Coming back tomorrow | Cash waiting on the pads from offline earnings (up to 2 hours' worth), the next zone, the rare boss that was too strong yesterday, the rebirth bar, and getting even with whoever stole from you. |

**What a second player adds:** somebody to steal from and guard against, a row of other players' bosses to want, and a shared rush when a high-tier boss appears on a pad.

**The progression engine:** every finished trip adds a boss that earns cash; cash buys Strength and Speed; those raise Power faster; more Power opens further zones with new creatures, a new hazard and longer, riskier carries. Rebirth restarts it faster with one more spot.

## Systems and numbers
**Every number in this section is an on-paper draft.** Nothing has been run in Studio. The scripter builds from the tables; the game-designer measures earn rates, fight lengths and times in play mode and reports them against these tables. All tunable values live in one config module.

Money is written `$` for the in-game cash and `R$` for Robux.

### 1. The timing ring and the three-punch combo
**Rule (manager, 2026-11-25: punches in threes, a timing ring on the bag and in fights, and "make sure the punches against bag or boss are timed against this", meaning the combo recording, reference E, on the listening page; the manager then chose its no-breath version, F, as the burst voice).** Punching is always the same action, on the bag and in boss fights: a ring closes on the Huss button and the player taps **once** as it lands. That one tap is graded (Perfect, Good or Miss). A Perfect or a Good launches one **"huss huss huss"**: a burst of three punches at the rhythm of the manager's recording, the first as the tap lands, the second 0.213 s later, the third 0.188 s after that (about five a second; the whole burst lasts 0.61 s, as recorded). The third punch is the big one and counts double. When the burst ends, the next ring starts closing. (`TEAM CALL`: one graded tap per burst, because three separate taps 0.2 s apart in 45 to 110 ms windows are not playable on a phone. The rhythm is fixed to the recording; the player times the ring, the burst follows.)

**One "huss huss huss" (one combo) in the rules:** one graded tap followed by three punches landing at 0, 0.213 and 0.401 s, worth 1 + 1 + 2 = 4 punches at Good and 2 + 2 + 4 = 8 at Perfect. A Miss launches no burst.

| Value | Draft |
|---|---|
| The burst (fixed, never changed by Speed) | three punches at 0, 0.213 and 0.401 s after the tap; 0.61 s until the next ring starts |
| Ring close time (Speed level 1) | 1.20 s (level 10: 0.50 s; table in system 2) |
| One combo cycle | ring close time + 0.61 s: 1.81 s at Speed 1, 1.11 s at Speed 10 |
| Perfect window (bag) | within 45 ms of the ring landing, either side |
| Good window (bag) | within 110 ms, either side |
| Miss | a tap outside the Good window, or no tap before the ring has passed by 110 ms. No burst; the ring restarts after the same 0.61 s |
| Grade value | Perfect x2, Good x1, Miss x0, applied to all three punches of the burst |
| Third punch of the burst | its grade value x2 (the "big huss") |
| Taps during a burst | ignored, no penalty |

- **What Punch Speed shortens** (`TEAM CALL`): the ring's close time, which is the wait between bursts. It never changes the burst itself: the burst always plays at the recorded rhythm, so every punch in the game sounds and looks like the manager's "huss huss huss". The windows are in milliseconds and do not shrink with Speed (on the bag they never change; in fights they narrow only by the difficulty band, system 4).
- The three combo pips fill with the three punches of the burst. The avatar throws its three jabs on the same three beats.
- **Sound** (for the audio-specialist, `sound-direction.md`): every landed burst plays **reference F** (`f_group1_no_breath.wav`: E, the first group of three husses, with its first 18 ms of breath trimmed, same rhythm; the manager heard it: "combo f sounds good"), as one sound, so picture and sound share its timing (the punches are scheduled to the recording's rhythm, 0.213 s and then 0.188 s apart). A Perfect adds its sting on top. **Single husses** use **A, huss 4 levelled**, with **A, huss 1 as cut** as a second take to alternate: the owner's plain punch at a thief, and anywhere else one punch is thrown alone.
- A "typical player" in the tables below lands 45% Perfect, 45% Good and 10% Miss: 5.4 punches' worth per combo on average. This is an assumption for arithmetic only.

**The server decides:** the ring schedule (it knows each player's Speed level, so it knows the earliest the next tap can come: one full cycle after the last) and the value of each burst. The client judges the tap against the ring it drew and reports the grade and the tap time. The server rejects taps that arrive faster than the cycle allows (20% tolerance), taps when the player is not at a bag or in a fight, and caps the rate. A cheating client can claim Perfects; the gain over a typical player is bounded at about 1.5x (8 against 5.4) and is on the exploit review list.
**Saved:** nothing.

**Auto Punch (pass, manager asked for it; accepted as is, 2026-11-25).** A toggle on the bag sign. Same limits: only on the owner's bag pad, only while in the game, never in fights, never offline.

**The server decides:** every Power gain, every level bought (cost checked and taken on the server), whether the player is on their own bag pad. Only the owner can use a base's bag.
**Saved:** Power, Strength level, Speed level.

### 3. Power

Typical Power per second on the bag = 5.4 per combo / combo cycle x Strength value (system 2's table). At Speed 1, Strength 1 that is about 3.0 a second, 180 a minute.

### 4. Boss fights
**Rule.** Stand in a boss's ring and press Huss to start. The player is held in place, the camera frames the boss, and the same timing ring runs, with the same one-tap "huss huss huss" burst (system 1). Each punch of a landed burst takes health off the boss. Each Miss lets the boss hit back and costs one of three hearts. Empty the boss's health bar to win; lose all three hearts and you lose. A boss above your Power can be beaten, but it gets much harder the further above you it is (manager, 2026-11-25): it has more health relative to your punch, so you need more bursts, and the ring's windows get narrower, so each tap is harder to land, and you still only have three hearts.

| Value | Draft |
|---|---|
| Boss health | 10 x the boss's Power |
| Damage per burst | your Power x 4 on a Good, x 8 on a Perfect (1 + 1 + 2 punches, x grade value), applied punch by punch at the burst's three beats |
| Hearts | 3 per fight; a Miss costs one |
| Ring close time and burst | your Punch Speed level's ring, as on the bag; the burst is always 0.61 s |
| Leaving | a Run button ends the fight as a loss |

Difficulty comes from the ratio r = boss Power / your Power, read when the fight starts and fixed for that fight.

| Band | r | Number colour over the boss | Good window | Perfect window | Taps (bursts) to win, all Goods | All Perfects |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Easy | up to 0.5 | green | 130 ms | 55 ms | 1 to 2 | 1 |
| Fair | over 0.5 to 1.0 | green | 110 ms | 45 ms | 2 to 3 | 1 to 2 |
| Hard | over 1.0 to 1.5 | yellow | 80 ms | 30 ms | 3 to 4 | 2 |
| Very hard | over 1.5 to 2.0 | orange | 60 ms | 22 ms | 4 to 5 | 2 to 3 |
| Brutal | over 2.0 to 3.0 | red | 45 ms | 16 ms | 5 to 8 | 3 to 4 |
| Near impossible | over 3.0 | red with a skull | 35 ms | 12 ms | 8 and up (r = 5 is 13) | 4 and up (r = 5 is 7) |

- The tap counts follow from the health and damage rules above: taps with all Goods = 2.5 x r rounded up, with all Perfects = 1.25 x r rounded up. At equal Power and Speed 1 a fight of all Goods is 3 taps, about 5.4 seconds (three cycles of 1.81 s), the same length as the earlier draft. Because a fight is now a few taps, each tap matters more: a Miss costs a heart out of three, and the band's narrow windows are what make high bosses hard (`TEAM CALL`: 3 hearts kept; measure the win rate per band in play mode).
- There is no ceiling: any boss can be attempted. Windows never go below the last row.
- **Losing costs time only** (`TEAM CALL`): the player is knocked back out of the ring and down for 2 seconds, the boss's health resets, and that boss cannot be challenged again by that player for 5 seconds. No Power, cash or boss is lost.
- Winning: the boss shrinks and lands on the player's head, carried (system 6). There is no cash for the win itself; bosses pay at base.
- **A boss emptied on punch 1 or 2 of a burst** (build plan B22): the fight is won on that beat. The later beats of that burst deal nothing and get no contact effects (the avatar's remaining jabs play without contact), and the burst's sound plays to its end while the defeat starts.
- A player who is carrying a boss cannot start a fight.
- Every player has their own fight with a boss (their own health bar), so nobody blocks anybody and nobody can steal a kill.

**How the boss fights back: small animations with its own body (manager, 2026-11-25: "should have small fighting animations too. and if its like a scorpion it should use its tail to stin").** Every boss moves in a fight, and it fights with the body it has: a scorpion stings with its tail and punches with its pincers, a shark bites, a ram headbutts. The moves are small and cheap, as the manager said "small": short tweens of the boss's moving groups (body, head, two fists and one signature part such as a tail, horns, jaw, wings, extra heads or tentacles; `style-guide.md` section 5, "How they are made"), with no skinned rig. The 36 creatures share 7 body types (`TEAM CALL`), and the moves are made once per body type, written per group as offsets from that group's own pivot so one set fits a Common and a Mythic. Each creature's one signature move (the boss's attack) is its body type's attack applied to its own signature part. Both tables are in Content.

| Moment | What the player sees the boss do | Length (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| On its pad, nobody fighting it | Idle, as in `style-guide.md` section 5: a bob, the head bobbing the other way, three short jabs every 2.5 to 4 s | loop |
| Fight starts, and between punches | Guard: turns to face the player, fists (or claws, tail, hood) up, a small bounce in time with the ring | loop for the fight |
| Punches 1 and 2 of a Good burst (at 0 and 0.213 s) | Hit reaction each: head snaps back, body squashes to 0.9 tall and 1.1 wide, white flash, cube burst | 0.15 s each |
| Punches 1 and 2 of a Perfect burst | The same, twice as far, and the boss slides back half a step | 0.2 s each |
| Punch 3, the big huss (at 0.401 s) | The largest reaction: knocked back a full step, staggers, its fists drop for a beat (bigger again on a Perfect) | 0.3 s |
| A Miss (the player loses a heart) | The boss's attack: its signature move, aimed at the player, inside the 0.61 s where a burst would have been. 0.15 s wind-up so it reads, then the strike; the avatar flinches and a heart breaks on the strike | 0.45 s |
| The player loses the last heart | The signature move once more, bigger; the avatar is knocked down for the 2 s knockdown; the boss gives a short gloat (fists up, or its own version) and goes back to idle | 1.0 s |
| Boss beaten | Defeat: it wobbles, its fists drop, it flops down, flashes white, then shrinks and hops onto the player's head (system 6) | 0.8 s |
| Carried on a head | No fight moves: only the carry rock from `style-guide.md` section 5 | loop |
| On a spot at a base | The idle at half size, as the style guide says; never a fight move or a signature move | loop |

- **No telegraph, no slam.** The fight system has no boss attack the player has to dodge: the boss strikes only as the result of a Miss. The 0.15 s wind-up is there so the strike reads, not as a warning. If a slam is ever added, it uses this wind-up.
- **Animations never hold up the ring.** They play over it; they never pause or delay the next punch. A reaction still playing when the next result arrives is cut and replaced by the new one.
- **Other players watching** (`TEAM CALL`): each fighter sees the boss react to their own fight. A player who is not fighting that boss sees reactions to the results the server sends to nearby clients, at most one reaction every 0.3 s per boss, so a crowded pad does not jitter.
- **Cost** (`TEAM CALL`): fight moves play only on bosses on boss pads within 150 studs of the camera. Placed bosses (up to 136 in view of the bases) play only the half idle, and stand still beyond 150 studs. The performance-optimizer may tune the distances and reports the frame time measured.
- The avatar's own moves (the three-jab rhythm, the big third punch, the flinch, the knockdown) are in Content, "Avatar animations". Each body type's attack gets one sound (`sound-direction.md`, the audio-specialist adds it).

**The server decides:** r and the band at fight start, boss health, damage per reported tap (the burst's three punches are applied on the server's schedule), hearts, win or loss, and that the player is in the ring and not carrying. Same punch-rate checks as the bag. **The animations are client-side dressing of the server's results:** the server sends each result (grade landed, heart lost, win, loss) and clients play the matching move. No animation decides or delays a result, a hit check or the ring.
**Saved:** nothing during a fight. A disconnect is a loss. A win adds the zone to the saved "zones beaten" list (system 14) if it is not on it yet.

Why this rule: it never takes away Robux-bought cash that is still in the player's hands, and it cannot be used to carry earned cash through a rebirth.

## Economy and monetization
**Currencies.** One soft currency, cash (`$`), and one stat, Power. Robux buys things directly; there is no premium currency. Spin tickets are a count, not a currency: they only come from the spin products and are only spent on spins.

**Sources and sinks**
| | New player (minute 5) | Late player (zone 6) | Repeats without limit? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Source of Power: bag punches | about 3 to 5 a second | tens of thousands a second | Limited by the punch rate the server allows |
| Source of cash: placed bosses | about $10 to $20 a second | $350,000 a second and up | Limited by spot count and pad caps |
| Source of cash: selling a boss | $20 to $80 a sale | 20 x its income | Limited by trip time; worth 20 seconds of one boss |
| Source of cash: offline | up to 2 hours of income | the same rule | Capped |
| Source of cash: cash packs (Robux) | at least $2,500 | 15 minutes to 12 hours of income | Paid |
| Source of bosses: fights; steals; Lucky Spin; Starter Pack | | | Fights limited by pads and spots; steals move a boss, never make one; the pack once |
| Sink of cash: Punch Strength | $150 to $1,800 | up to $4.3B a level | 29 purchases |
| Sink of cash: Punch Speed | $100 to $1,600 | up to $6.5M | 9 purchases |
| Sink of cash: boss upgrades | $30 and up | 14,519 x base income per boss | 19 levels per boss |
| Sink of everything: rebirth | | resets earned cash, Power, Strength, Speed and every boss beaten or stolen; the buyer's own bought bosses (at their levels), unspent bought cash, tickets, Shield Breaker charges and passes stay | 10 times |

**The curve** is the set of tables in "Systems and numbers" (3, 5, 2, 9, 12). Milestones: first upgrade 1:27; zone 2 at 4 minutes; zone 3 at 11; zone 4 at 30; zone 5 at 1 hour; zone 6 at 2 hours; first rebirth at 3 to 4 hours. All on paper.

**Anything random:** rolling pads (free, odds in system 5) and the Lucky Boss Spin (paid, odds and expected value in system 13).

**Events:** none in version 1. An event later would bring its own currency and its own sink, so it cannot flood cash.

**Monetization: exactly what the manager asked for.** Passes, cash packs, a lucky boss spin with odds shown, a starter pack, an auto-punch pass for the bag, and (the manager's "maybe") a perfect auto-punch pass; things that work against other players may be sold, attacking products included; what is bought with Robux stays through rebirth for its buyer; bought bosses can be stolen, as in the genre's leading games. The full list with prices is in system 13, **19 items: 10 game passes** (9 perk passes, two of them attacking: Quick Hands, Iron Grip; and the Starter Pack, built as a pass) **and 9 developer products** (4 cash packs, 3 spin bundles, Instant Shield, Shield Breaker). All are created under the group that owns the game. No ads, no premium currency, no subscription, no season pass, no boosts, no trading, no gifting.

**Policy checks done:** paid random items, `vault/roblox-docs/paid-random-items-policy.md` (docs, checked 2026-11-11, re-check by 2027-02-11 and before release), and the docs page and DevForum post read again on 2026-11-25 for stealing (`vault/observations/2026-11-25-steal-games-paid-items-and-stealing.md`, section 5): silent on theft, so the bought-boss steal guard in system 10 stays `UNVERIFIED` until Roblox is asked. Offer wording, `vault/roblox-docs/monetization-presenting-offers.md` (docs, checked 2026-11-19). Passes and products, `vault/roblox-docs/monetization-fees-devex-passes-products.md` (docs, checked 2026-11-10).

**Cautions (said once; the brief above is written as the manager asked).**
1. **The Lucky Boss Spin is a paid random item.** Roblox policy requires every outcome and its real odds shown before purchase, totalling 100%, and that restricted players cannot use it. System 13 is written to that. A forum announcement is reported to add that every outcome must give some benefit; ours always gives a boss. Source: `vault/roblox-docs/paid-random-items-policy.md` (docs).
2. **Bought bosses can be stolen, and a steal may count as a trade.** Bought bosses are stealable because the manager said to do what the genre's leading games do, and the research found no paid-unit protection in them (`vault/observations/2026-11-25-steal-games-paid-items-and-stealing.md`, medium-low confidence: indirect, from guide sites, nothing played). Roblox's paid random items page says players for whom `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` is false must not trade the results of paid random items, and a Roblox clarification on the DevForum (2026-05-26) says the flag restricts trading of all paid items, so every boss bought with Robux is covered, the Starter Pack's Headbutt Ram as well as spin bosses. No official source says whether a steal is trading (checked 2026-11-25, see the docs digest). The brief takes the careful reading (system 10: a bought boss cannot change hands when the flag is false for either player), and the paste-ready question for Roblox is in `vault/roblox-docs/paid-random-items-policy.md`. **Before release, ask Roblox;** if Roblox says stealing is not trading, the guard is removed. Sources: `vault/roblox-docs/paid-random-items-policy.md`, the docs page and the DevForum post named in the observation (docs). Losing a bought boss to a thief is the strongest complaint risk in the game; the shop must never promise that a bought boss is safe, and the BOUGHT tag means "from the shop", not "protected". Bought cash that was spent is not returned at rebirth, and a bought boss that was sold or stolen is not returned either; the rebirth screen and the shop say so in plain words, because Roblox's monetization page says players must never be misled about a purchase: `vault/roblox-docs/monetization-presenting-offers.md` (docs).
3. **Selling protection next to stealing.** Roblox's page says to avoid pushy or urgent wording with younger users, so Instant Shield is sold only where the player goes looking for it, its shop card and the lock board beside the lock pad, and never by a pop-up or on or beside a steal or "shield broken" alert (system 10). Same source (docs). The vault's one record of a stealing game drew complaints about being targeted, and protection was what sold best: `vault/patterns/interact-with-other-players-progress.md` (multi-source).
4. **Attacking products raise the complaint risk.** Shield Breaker, Quick Hands and Iron Grip are built because the manager asked for attacking products. The vault's stealing-game record is that complaints were about being targeted, and that losing progress to others can drive away new and young players: `vault/patterns/interact-with-other-players-progress.md` (multi-source, none of it from the manager's own games). What limits it here: one break per base per 5 minutes, the unbreakable joining shield and Instant Shield, the owner's chase speed, the crate and offline players out of reach. Bought bosses are no longer out of reach (caution 2), so a paid attack can now take a paid boss. The urgent-wording rule in caution 3 applies to both sides: the breaker button says "Break shield R$39" and nothing more, and a broken shield never opens an Instant Shield offer. Paying on both sides of one fight (breaker against Instant Shield) is the part most likely to draw complaints; the numbers to watch after release are D1 retention and the feedback tab, through `/retro`.
5. **Auto Punch and Perfect Auto Punch remove the main action for their buyers.** One studied team refused to sell an automatic version of their core action because it hollowed out the loop; another sold it as a best seller: `vault/patterns/sell-speed-and-expression-keep-basics-free.md` (multi-source). Built as asked. Auto Punch stays below a typical hand (4 against 5.4 per burst); Perfect Auto Punch (8) beats a typical hand and only equals a player who never misses, so with it the bag no longer rewards skill; fights still do.
6. **Exploit risk.** Stealing (duplication, teleport-steals), client-judged timing (claimed Perfects), the bought mark and its buyer id (anything that could mark earned or stolen things as bought by you), the paid-item trading check on steals, the bought-cash number and Shield Breaker receipts are the highest-risk parts. All go to the qa-exploit-reviewer before release.

- Pets, cosmetics, base decoration, chat commands, private-server perks.
- Auto-collect and Auto Punch in fights.
- Boss moves beyond the small set in system 4: no boss slam or telegraph to dodge, no projectiles, no skinned rigs.

## Never
- No stealing on the lane. Stealing is at bases only.
- No stealing from a player who is offline. A base is in the world only while its owner is in the server.
- No attacking product ever works on an offline player, on the 60 s joining shield, or on an Instant Shield.
- No boss bought with Robux (a spin boss or the Starter Pack's Ram) changes hands in a steal when either player's `IsPaidItemTradingAllowed` is false (until Roblox says otherwise).
- Rebirth never takes away a pass, an unused ticket, a Shield Breaker charge, a bought boss its buyer still owns, or unspent bought cash.
- Nothing in the shop or on a tag says a bought boss is safe from stealing.
- Nothing copied from other games: no names, creatures, layouts, assets or scripts. Reference screenshots are for technique.
- No toy-brick-brand look, ever: no round-stud toy bricks, no minifigure, no brand-like logo or word. Roblox studs and the Roblox avatar only.
- No creature that resembles a known character. Ember Drake has no flame-tipped tail.
- No built boss that does not match its approved picture. Chomp Frog, Snap Scorpion, Bit Shark, Frost Byte, Ember Drake (with its rock-club tail) and Star Golem are built to look like `concepts/brick-brawl/creatures.png`; a model that does not is not accepted, and the other 30 are not made until the manager has seen the first six built.
- No boss animation that decides anything: fight moves are client-side dressing of results the server already decided, and they never pause or delay the ring.
- The Lucky Spin never runs without its outcomes and odds on screen, never has an outcome that gives nothing, never has odds that differ from what is shown, and is never shown to a player Roblox marks as restricted from paid random items.
- No false discounts, no countdowns that restart, no "last chance" or other urgent wording, no prompt stacked on another, no prompt during a fight, a carry or a steal alert.
- No Extra Spots offer while carrying, and none while an alert is on: it is offered only on the spots-full sign by the crate and on the "Spots full" toast when taking a boss out of the crate with every spot full (system 7).
- No buy button on the lock pad: stepping on it only raises the shield. Long Lock and Instant Shield are on the lock board.
- No hazard that can be walked around: each sits in its zone's walled 36-wide throat and always leaves a way through inside it.
- The client never decides Power, cash, a fight's result, a roll, a steal or a purchase.
- No picture of anything that is not in the content tables.
- No publishing, pricing, settings or pushing by the team.

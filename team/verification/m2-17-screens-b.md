## How they are driven

Sends only: `Shop.Buy {key}` (Quick Hands, Long Lock, Instant Shield), `Shield.UseCharge {plotId}` or `Shield.BuyBreaker {plotId}`, `Base.Sell {}`, `Base.Swap {spotIndex}`. No price or amount is sent.
